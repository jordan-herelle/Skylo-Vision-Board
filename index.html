01 · The Proposal
What this deck is

This deck is a proposal to engage in enhancing Skylo's identity, in support of the company's new messaging, voice, and communications plan.

Skylo Today

A logo, applied unevenly.

Skylo's existing brand guidelines are essentially logo guidelines. A mark, a color, a couple of typographic rules. That's it.

Social. Inconsistent feel post-to-post, no templated rhythm.
Website. Clear, but cold; not the brand a consumer would feel.
Press & analyst kits. No system for media or analysts to grab and reuse.
Motion & technical diagrams. Case-by-case, not from a system.
Decks & collateral. Re-litigated every time.
What We Need

A unique brand vibe. Something people feel.

An identity that goes beyond the logo so people feel something unique when they see Skylo, anywhere they see it.

One system. Voice, photography, motion, color, type. For any partner or in-house designer to build from.
Consistent in social, web, press kits. And in every motion graphic and technical diagram an analyst grabs.
Premium without shouting. The simplicity of the MWC work, stretched into a full brand world.
Owned by Skylo. A portfolio of assets that compounds, not a one-off project.

08 · Today vs. Tomorrow

Here's what changes, discipline by discipline. Today: ad-hoc decks, hard-to-read collateral, stock imagery that fights the brand. Tomorrow: a system Skylo's designer can scale, with the consumer feel of MWC carried into every touchpoint.

01 · Discipline
Brand Guide
From
Pieces of identity scattered across decks, no single source of truth. New work re-litigates basics every time.
To
A definitive Brand Vibe Guide. Voice, type, color, motion, photography direction. That your designer and any partner builds from.
02 · Discipline
Photography
From
Off-the-shelf stock that doesn't read as Skylo. Recognizable hands holding recognizable phones. Often hard to read against type.
To
A bespoke, human-centered library shot to direction. Everyday, government, adventure. That Skylo owns end-to-end. (Like the imagery you're seeing in this doc.)
03 · Discipline
Motion Graphics
From
Static slides and one-off animations. Every video starts from scratch.
To
A repeatable motion system. Brand bumpers, transitions, social cut-downs. So video is a system, not a heroic effort.
04 · Discipline
Social Asset Library
From
Social posts assembled per-launch. Inconsistent rhythm, inconsistent feel.
To
A production-ready template set across LinkedIn, IG, X, and TikTok. Branded, sized, editable, ready for your team.
05 · Discipline
Video Templates
From
Hero videos that live or die with one production. Hard to repeat.
To
An After Effects + Premiere template system. Hero, explainer, product demos, social. So the brand has a kinetic standard, not a kinetic gamble.

10 · Benchmarks
Starting point · MWC
We know the simplicity and consumer feel of Skylo's advertising at MWC landed. That's the bar. The brands below show how to stretch that feel into something more emotional, more lifestyle, more premium, without losing the clarity that already works for you.
Adventure tech
Garmin inReach
We chose Garmin because they proved off-grid connectivity is a category people pay for. We think they over-index on rugged spec and under-deliver on emotion. A lane Skylo can own with a more human story.
Emotional storytelling
Patagonia
We chose Patagonia because photography is the brand and type stays out of the way. We think they show how a technical company can feel like a worldview. Exactly the move Skylo is making.
Premium technical
Arc'teryx
We chose Arc'teryx because their identity is monolithic and their materials feel earned. We think they are the proof that technical credibility can become quiet desire. Premium without ever shouting.
Tech-as-lifestyle
Apple · AirTag launch
We chose Apple because they sell invisible technology with cinematic film. We think they teach us the reveal language. Black backgrounds, small product, big emotion. For a layer that's literally invisible.

12 · Our Process

Think of us as scale and senior creative support for Skylo's in-house team. Darlene owns the brand; we extend her capacity with a flexible pod of creative directors, designers, motion artists, photographers, and copywriters. The engagement flexes up or down as needs change, with the appropriate time built in to get the brand foundation right.

01 · Foundation

Take the time to build the vibe.

Brand Vibe Guide, photography direction, motion principles, voice — and the start of an asset library that will compound for years. We don't rush this; the foundation is what every other deliverable rests on.

02 · Production

Scale alongside Darlene.

Darlene briefs, we deliver. Decks, social, video, MWC + ad creative, web. Turned around on SLA, all built from the system, all in service of what's on her plate.

03 · Compounding

Grow the asset library.

Every project adds to a living library of templates, photography, and motion components. That portfolio is foundational, not nice-to-have. It's what makes the next brief faster and sharper.

13 · The Engagement

A small set of milestone meetings to move from this doc to a launched system, with Parth involved early and signing off at every key stage. Nothing moves forward without alignment. The pacing flexes to give the brand foundation the time it deserves.

Meeting 01 · Parth Kickoff

Vision + Creative Bar

60 min · Week 1

A focused 1-hour kickoff with Parth to lock the vision, the bar, and the tone for the brand foundation. Structured question set; signed creative brief by end of meeting.

Parth Sign-Off
Meeting 02 · Review

Brand Vibe + Photography

90 min · Week 4–5

We present the Brand Vibe Guide v1 and the photography direction (storyboards + shoot plan). Parth and the team react, we refine, we lock production.

Parth Sign-Off
Meeting 03 · Launch

System + Web Refresh

60 min · Week 8–10

Motion system, social library, video templates, MWC + advertising creative, and a light refresh of the website handed over. The team runs from here. We stay on retainer and scale up or down with needs.

Parth Sign-Off
Inside Meeting 01 · Questions for Parth

The structured question list.

A short, focused set built to extract the vision, taste, and bar in a single hour. The answers shape the entire mood board phase that follows.

  1. Brands you love and why?
  2. Brands you can't stand and why?
  3. Where is Skylo 5–10 years from now?
  4. Best creative work you've ever seen?
  5. Worst creative work you've ever seen?
